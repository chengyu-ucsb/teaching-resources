# Teaching resources

Class activities and tools for communication courses at the University of Cincinnati, by Chengyu Fang.

Site: https://chengyu-ucsb.github.io/teaching-resources/

| Folder | What it is | Course |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `rate-race/` | The Rate Race, an interactive simulation of classical management | COMM 3008 |
| `scorecard/` | Audience scorecard for final presentations. Scores save to a Google Sheet. | COMM 5009, COMM 7007 |

The Tallgrass crisis simulation (COMM 5009) is linked from the home page and lives in its own repository, `tallgrass-crisis-simulation`.

## Scorecard

- `scorecard/#5009` opens the COMM 5009 client pitch scorecard.
- `scorecard/#7007` opens the COMM 7007 final presentation scorecard.
- Team, client, and presenter names come from the Setup tab of the score sheet. Edit them there, not in the code.
- The scorecard is not linked from the home page. Share its address directly with the people who will score.
